## 1. Context of Engagement

Mohamad Hafez, the founder and owner of **Pistachio Cafe** (Westville) and **Pistachio Cafe 2** (Downtown New Haven), contacted Saif Uddin regarding severe operational frustrations and mounting business dissatisfaction with **Owner.com** (the third-party restaurant SaaS provider currently hosting and managing `https://pistachiocafe.com/` and their mobile application).

Owner.com has been charging **$500 per month ($6,000 annually)** for their platform. Despite these ongoing payments:
1. Restaurant revenues have experienced a sharp, sustained downturn.
2. Pistachio Cafe 2 (Chapel St) was rendered completely invisible across Google Local 3-Pack rankings (0% visibility for high-intent keywords like "breakfast near me", "coffee near me", and "brunch near me").
3. Unbranded organic customer acquisition ground to a near halt (only 9% unbranded search visits, generating a dismal $0.51 revenue per SEO visit compared to the industry median of $5.26).
4. The client felt held hostage by Owner.com: if the $500/month subscription is canceled, Owner.com retains full ownership of the digital ordering portal and mobile app, threatening immediate operational paralysis unless an independent, native replacement is fully operational.

To resolve this crisis, Mohamad engaged Saif Uddin to engineer a complete, seamless digital transformation: replacing Owner.com with an in-house, high-performance Headless website connected directly to their **Square POS** system, restoring full control of their domain, marketing assets, customer data, and online revenue.
